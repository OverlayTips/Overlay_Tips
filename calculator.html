What to do

Getting the most out of it
  • Bonus bets: go for higher odds. The stake isn't returned, so longer odds usually convert a bigger share. Around $4–$8 tends to be the sweet spot.
  • Keep the gap small. The closer the back and lay (or both bookie) odds are, the less you lose. Check the odds right before you place both bets.
  • Place both bets back to back. Odds move fast. If one side changes, put the new odds in and re-check before you place the second bet.
  • Back / back needs two outcomes only. Use it for markets like head-to-head or line betting, with the two sides at different bookies.
