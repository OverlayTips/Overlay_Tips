🔥 Hot props

Hit rate = games the player went over the line, out of the last 10 (last 5, and vs tonight's opponent this season). Value picks lean mostly on the market (Pinnacle's sharp price when there is one, otherwise the Aussie bookies' consensus with their margin taken off), adjusted a little for player form (recent games count more), then compare that to the chance the best price needs. Anything that looks like more than +15% is left out: that's nearly always a stale or wrong price. Fair price = that rated chance turned into odds (1 ÷ chance), so a price above fair is value. Value = expected return per $1, e.g. a 66% chance at $1.65 = +9%. Only lines priced by 3+ bookies, up to $3. Past form isn't a guarantee, so check the price is still there before you bet.
