Hit rate = games the player went over the line, out of the last 10 (last 5, and vs tonight's opponent this season). Value picks blend player form (last 10 + season, nudged toward 50% when there aren't many games) with what the bookies as a group think, then compare that to the chance the best price needs. Fair price = that rated chance turned into odds (1 ÷ chance), so a price above fair is value. Value = expected return per $1, e.g. a 66% chance at $1.65 = +9%.
