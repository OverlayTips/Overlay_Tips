About

Back the value.

Overlay Tips is an Australian sports analysis page that identifies value betting opportunities, where the available odds exceed our assessed fair price. Every selection is published, and every result is tracked and reported with full transparency.

The method

Hit rate vs odds

Every tip starts with numbers. We compare how often something actually happens (recent hit rates) with the chance the bookie's odds imply. When the real chance is higher than the odds suggest, that's an overlay.

The record

Every bet posted

Tips go out before the game with a unit size and the reasoning. Results go up after, losses included. Nothing gets deleted, so the record you see is the full record.

The sports

Wherever the value is

Horse racing, NBA, NBL, AFL, NRL, NFL and EPL. We shop prices across bookies and only tip when there's a real edge, not just because there's a game on.

Analysed tips, not guaranteed wins. Results are shown in units (1U = your standard bet) so they work for any bankroll. Only bet what you can afford to lose.
