How a round works

  • Every NBA day (Melbourne time) is a round. Pick 5 players from that day's games.
  • Make one of them your Overlay Pick (★). It scores double.
  • Picks lock when the first game starts. Change them as often as you like before then.
  • Multipliers are locked at lock too, so late price moves don't change anything.

Odds multiplier

From the player's price to score 20+ points at the Aussie bookies (the middle price across them).

"est." means no bookie priced 20+ for that player, so it's worked out from his main points line.

Scoring

Player score = base points × multiplier. Overlay Pick = base points × multiplier × 2. Your round score is your 5 players added up.

Example: 18 points, 6 rebounds, 4 assists, 1 steal = 18 + 9 + 6 + 3 = 36 base. 20+ points paying $4.50 = 2x, so 72. As your Overlay Pick, 144.

Fair play

  • One entry per member per round.
  • A player who doesn't play scores 0. No swaps after lock.
  • Ties go to the highest single-player score, then the earliest entry.
  • Overlay Tips can update these rules between rounds, with notice to members.
