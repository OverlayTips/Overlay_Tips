Value picks from Hot stats

These blend each player's last 10 games and season form with what the bookies as a group think, then compare that to what the best price needs.

Brice Sensabaugh 3+ rebounds, $1.87 PointsBet (Nuggets @ Jazz)

Sensabaugh has pulled down 3+ boards in 12 of his last 15, including all of his last 5, averaging 3.7. He had 4 at the Lakers last time out in just 17 minutes. The big one is Lauri Markkanen being out with a hand fracture: Sensabaugh averages 4.3 rebounds without him compared with 2.8 when they play together, and he's landed this in 11 of 11 games without him. Jaren Jackson Jr. is out for Utah too. PointsBet's $1.87 beats the $1.75 or so at most bookies; it needs a 53% chance and we rate it about 61%. Form, role and price all line up.

Dyson Daniels 8+ rebounds, $1.93 TABtouch (Hawks @ Celtics)

Daniels has 8+ rebounds in 10 of his last 15, including all of his last 5, averaging 8.2, and grabbed 14 against the Pistons last time out. With CJ McCollum out he's averaging 10.7 compared with 7.4 alongside him, and he's 3 from 3 in those games. Big man Jock Landale is out for Atlanta as well. The knocks: that last game was a heavy 38 minutes, and he had 5, 6 and 6 in his last three against Boston, who sit around the middle of the league defending guards on the glass. TABtouch's $1.93 is in line with the market; it needs a 52% chance and we rate it about 55%. A lean rather than a big play.

Quentin Grimes 6+ rebounds + assists, $2.10 Sportsbet (Lakers @ Spurs)

Grimes has hit 6+ rebounds + assists in 5 of his last 7, including all of his last 5, averaging 7.9. His role has grown, with about 32 minutes a game over his last 7, up from 25 before that, and he had 11 against the Jazz last time out. The Spurs give up the 5th-most rebounds + assists to guards (+5%). On the other side, he projects for about 27.5 minutes off the bench and had just 2 and 3 in his last two games against San Antonio. Austin Reaves is listed out with a heel injury, which could open up more of the ball for Grimes, but wait for confirmation on Reaves. Sportsbet's $2.10 beats the $1.93 at most bookies; it needs a 48% chance and we rate it about 50%.

Jeremiah Fears 7+ assists, $2.23 Unibet (Pelicans @ Clippers)

Fears has 7+ assists in 8 of his last 15 but only 2 of his last 5, averaging 6.7. He dished 11 against the Suns last time out, though in a heavy 39 minutes. Dejounte Murray is out again, and Fears averages 7.5 assists without him compared with 3.6 alongside him, landing this in 7 of 10. His last three against the Clippers were 5, 2 and 3, so the head-to-head isn't kind. Unibet's $2.23 beats the $2.10 at most bookies; it needs a 45% chance and we rate it about 48%. There's value in the price, but keep the stake small.
