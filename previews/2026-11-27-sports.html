Javonte Williams 2+ receptions, $1.85 Unibet (Eagles @ Cowboys)

Unibet's $1.85 is well clear of the $1.68 at most bookies; it needs a 54% chance and we rate it about 58%. Keep the stake small.

Patrick Mahomes 2+ passing TDs, $2.30 Sportsbet (Chiefs @ Bills)

A lean rather than a big play.
