Value picks from Hot stats

These blend each player's last 10 games and season form with what the bookies as a group think, then compare that to what the best price needs. All of today's picks come from the Pistons and Magic game.

Daniss Jenkins 4+ rebounds, $1.77 Neds (Pistons @ Magic)

Jenkins has hit 4+ boards in 7 of his last 8 and 4 of his last 5, averaging 4.5, and had 5 at the Hawks last time out in 37 minutes. His role has jumped, from about 25 minutes a game to about 37 over his last 8, and he projects for about 34.5 tonight. Duncan Robinson is out again, and without him Jenkins is averaging 4.5 rebounds compared with 3.6, landing it in all 4 games. Jalen Duren is also out with a hamstring strain, so there are boards to go around. The knock: he went 3, 1 and 0 in his last three against Orlando, though that was before his bigger role. Neds and Ladbrokes have $1.77 against about $1.58 at most bookies; it needs a 56% chance and we rate it about 61%.

Anthony Black 2+ threes, $2.10 TABtouch (Pistons @ Magic)

Black has made 2+ threes in 7 of his last 8 and 4 of his last 5, averaging 2.1, and hit 2 at the Wizards last time out in 34 minutes. He's playing about 35 minutes a game over his last 8, up from 28 before that, and with Paolo Banchero out (high ankle sprain) he should keep the ball in his hands. The catch is the matchup: the Pistons allow the 6th-fewest threes to guards (-13%), and he's landed it in just 1 of his last 3 against them. TABtouch and Unibet have $2.10 against about $1.97 at most bookies; it needs a 48% chance and we rate it about 50%. A small-stake lean.

Anthony Black 5+ assists, $2.02 Sportsbet (Pistons @ Magic)

Same story on the role, and Black has 5+ assists in 7 of his last 8 and 4 of his last 5, averaging 5.6. Last time out was a quiet one, just 2 at the Wizards. Detroit are a tough draw here, allowing the 2nd-fewest assists to guards (-19%), and he's gone 1, 1 and 3 in his last three against them. Sportsbet's $2.02 beats the $1.91 at most bookies; it needs a 50% chance and we rate it about 51%. Only a slim edge, so if you're having one Black bet, the threes rate a touch better.

Paul Reed 8+ points, $1.44 Neds (Pistons @ Magic)

Reed has scored 8+ in 14 of his last 15, including all of his last 5, averaging 12.1, and had 16 at the Hawks last time out in 25 minutes. Without Duncan Robinson he's averaging 12.8 compared with 11.3, landing it in all 4 games, and Jalen Duren being out should mean plenty of minutes. The warning sign is his record against Orlando: 0, 6 and 4 in his last three. The $1.44 is in line with the market rather than a bargain; it needs a 69% chance and we rate it about 70%.
