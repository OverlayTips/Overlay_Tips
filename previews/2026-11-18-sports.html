Luka Doncic 5+ threes, $1.91 Neds (Hornets @ Lakers)

Doncic has made 5+ threes in 9 of his last 10 and 4 of his last 5, averaging 5.8, and hit 5 at the Suns last time out in 35 minutes. Neds and Ladbrokes have $1.91 against around $1.80 elsewhere; it needs a 52% chance and we rate it about 58%.

Jaden McDaniels 9+ rebounds + assists, $1.78 Sportsbet (Trail Blazers @ Timberwolves)

McDaniels has cleared this in 9 of his last 10 and all of his last 5, averaging 10.2, and had 9 against Portland last night in 31 minutes. The Blazers give up the 5th-most rebounds + assists to forwards, and he's landed it in 2 of his last 3 against them (9, 9 and 8). It's the second night of a back-to-back, and $1.78 is only in line with the market, but it needs a 56% chance and we rate it about 60%.

Landry Shamet 9+ points, $1.93 Unibet (Knicks @ Raptors)

Shamet has hit 9+ in 7 of his last 10 and 4 of his last 5, averaging 11.5, and dropped 23 in Toronto on Sunday in 29 minutes. He's landed this in 2 of his last 3 against the Raptors, who are missing Kawhi Leonard and Nate Bittle. It shapes as a slow game, which takes a few shots away. Unibet's $1.93 beats the $1.80 everywhere else; it needs a 52% chance and we rate it about 54%.

Ryan Kalkbrenner 5+ rebounds, $1.98 Neds (Hornets @ Lakers)

Kalkbrenner has 5+ boards in only 5 of his last 10, but 4 of his last 5, and the reason is Dennis Schroder being out: he's averaging 7.8 without him and 4.3 with him, landing it in all 4 games Schroder has missed. Schroder is out again today, and the Lakers are without Walker Kessler. He only projects for about 19.5 minutes, so there's not a lot of margin. It needs a 51% chance and we rate it about 53%, so it's a small lean.

Victor Wembanyama 3+ assists, $1.72 Neds (Pistons @ Spurs)

Wemby has 3+ assists in 7 of his last 10 and 4 of his last 5, averaging 3.7, and had 6 at the Jazz last time out in 35 minutes. He's landed it in both of his last two against Detroit (3 and 4), and the Pistons are short, with Duren, Robinson, Holland II and Prince all out. The worry is the spread: a blowout could cut his minutes, and he projects for about 26.5. It needs a 58% chance and we rate it about 61%.
